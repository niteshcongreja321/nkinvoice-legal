Common Questions

Do I need an account?

No. NK Invoice works fully without an account, and your data stays on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want a backup and the same invoices on another device (Settings → Back up & sync).

How do I get my invoices on a new device?

Sign in on the old device first, so your data is backed up. On the new device, tap I already have an account on the welcome screen and sign in with the same Apple or Google account.

What does the free plan include?

Three invoices each calendar month, plus clients, saved items, payments, tax, export, and backup. NK Invoice Pro adds unlimited invoices, estimates, your logo, all four PDF styles, and Insights.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Restore purchases, or tap Restore on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Export data. You get a zip file with spreadsheets (CSV) and a PDF of every invoice.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, then go to Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and your backed-up data from our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
