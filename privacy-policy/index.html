NK Invoice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the NK Invoice iOS app ("App") handles, where it is stored, and who can see it. "We", "our", and "us" mean NK Genesis, who operates the App.

In Short

  • You can use the App without an account. Your data then stays on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in, your data is backed up to Supabase in Seoul, South Korea, so it can sync across your devices.
  • Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. We never see your payment card details.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who We Are

NK Invoice is an invoicing app for freelancers and small businesses. It helps you create invoices and estimates, keep track of clients, saved items, and payments, and export PDFs. The App is operated by NK Genesis.

Contact: nkinvoice@gmail.com

Using The App Without An Account

An account is optional. If you don't sign in, everything you enter (your business profile, logo, clients, invoices, estimates, line items, saved items, payments, and any images you attach) is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us.

The App still contacts Apple and RevenueCat to provide your subscription status. See "Subscriptions And Purchases" below.

Backup And Sync (Optional Account)

You can choose to sign in with Apple or Google to back up your data and sync it across your devices. If you do, we store:

  • Account details: your email address (with Sign in with Apple, this may be a private relay address) and the name Apple provides the first time you sign in.
  • Your business data: your business profile and logo, clients, invoices and estimates, line items, payments, saved items, and any image you attach to a document.

This data is stored with Supabase, our sign-in and database provider, on Amazon Web Services servers in Seoul, South Korea (region ap-northeast-2). Row-level security rules mean only your signed-in account can read your data.

Apple or Google confirms who you are when you sign in, under their own privacy policies. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Subscriptions And Purchases

NK Invoice Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple processes all payments. We never receive your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives:

  • An app user ID: a random anonymous ID, or your account's user ID if you are signed in.
  • Purchase data: your purchase history and transaction (receipt) data from Apple.
  • Basic device data: app version, iOS version, device model, locale or country, and IP address.

This information is used only to provide and restore your subscription access. You can restore purchases and manage your subscription from the App's Settings or the NK Invoice Pro screen.

Other Features

  • Notifications: if you turn them on, reminders about overdue invoices and a reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled on your device. We don't use a push notification service, and nothing is sent to a server.
  • App lock: the optional Face ID or passcode lock uses Apple's LocalAuthentication. Your device does the check. The App never sees or stores biometric data.
  • PDFs and data export: PDFs and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose. Once you share or save a file, it is handled by your device's operating system and the app or service you pick.
  • Email and feedback: if you email us, including through "Feedback & support" in Settings, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. The feedback email is pre-filled with your app version, device model, iOS version, sync status, and locale. You can edit it before sending.

What We Don't Do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We never sell your data.

How We Use Information

  • Back up and sync your data when you are signed in.
  • Provide, check, and restore your NK Invoice Pro subscription.
  • Reply to support and feedback emails.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • Delete your account and data when you ask.

Sharing Information

We share information only with:

  • Supabase: sign-in and storage of synced data, if you sign in.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it. Apple also processes all App Store purchases.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status, as described above.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data Retention And Deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it in the App or delete the App.
  • Synced data: kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Signing out: removes the synced copy from that device. It stays in your account.
  • Deleting your account: in Settings, this permanently deletes your account and all its synced data from Supabase.
  • Subscription records: RevenueCat and Apple keep these under their own policies. To ask for the RevenueCat data tied to your app user ID to be deleted, email nkinvoice@gmail.com.

Your Rights

  • Access and correction: view and edit your data in the App.
  • Deletion: delete records in the App, or delete your account in Settings.
  • Portability: export your data as CSV files and PDFs from the App.
  • Requests: email nkinvoice@gmail.com for anything else, such as access to or deletion of data we hold about you. Depending on where you live, local law may give you other rights, and you can use them the same way.

Security

Synced data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by account sign-in and row-level security. On your device, you can add the optional Face ID or passcode lock. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

NK Invoice is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Processing

If you sign in, your synced data is stored in South Korea. Apple and RevenueCat may process subscription data in other countries where they operate. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from yours.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
